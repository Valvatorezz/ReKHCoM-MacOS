ReKHCoM for PS5

ReKHCoM: CC0 1.0 (LICENSE-ReKHCoM.md). The game code is the
matching decompilation of Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories by Pheenoh/khcom. No ROM is included:
copy your own cartridge dump to /data/ReKHCom.

Built into eboot.bin:

PS5 platform layer (Vulkan presentation, audio output, controllers, /data access)
  GNU GPL v3 or later (LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt)

RADV, Mesa's Vulkan driver for AMD GPUs (PS5 port by Mihawk-99)
  MIT License - https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa

Lapy (privilege helper, lapy.elf)
  MIT License (Lapy-MIT.txt)

SDL 2 - zlib License - https://www.libsdl.org
dlmalloc (Doug Lea) - public domain (CC0)
miniz - MIT License - https://github.com/richgel999/miniz (update download)
libcurl (curl license), OpenSSL (Apache-2.0), zlib, zstd, libpsl - PacBrew builds, used to check and download updates
